*For community admins. How-to.*

Make the tool carry your community's name and colour. No rebuild is needed. A change applies to everyone who opens the tool.

## What you need first

- An admin session. See [Set up the first admin](../first-admin/).
- For a logo: an SVG file up to 32 KB.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**. The **Workspace** tab is first.
2. Change any of these fields:
   - **Name**: up to 48 characters. Shown at the top of every page.
   - **Tagline**: up to 120 characters. Shown on the sign-in screen.
   - **Accent colour**: written as `#rrggbb`. It needs a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 on the light page. The readout under the field shows the ratio. Dark mode uses a lighter copy of your colour, shown as "Dark page, lightened copy".
   - **Logo**: choose an SVG file. Scripts, links and embedded content are refused. Choose **Remove logo** to go back to the built-in mark.
   - **Community paragraph**: up to 400 characters. Shown on the home page under For communities.
3. Choose **Save changes**. Choose **Discard changes** to go back to what is saved.

The tool also checks the name, tagline and paragraph against the same list of words the draft checker avoids. A field with such a word is refused, and the list "These fields need attention" links to it.

## If the save is refused

- "Someone else changed settings, reload to see their version. Your edits are not saved." Choose **Reload settings**, then make your change again. Saves use the version you last saw, so two admins cannot overwrite each other without notice.
- A logo message such as "That file is larger than 32 KB." or "Choose a file ending in .svg." Fix the file and choose it again.

## Check that it worked

- The name shows at the top of the app after saving, and the message "Settings saved" is announced.
- Open the public home page in a private window. Visitors can take up to five minutes to see the change, because the branding is cached for five minutes.
- The browser's install name follows the workspace name.

Settings also has an **Export** tab. It downloads `settings.json` with the name, wording, accent, logo and sign-in choice. The file holds no invite codes and no secrets.
